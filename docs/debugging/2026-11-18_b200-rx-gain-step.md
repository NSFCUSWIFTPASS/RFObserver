# B200mini receiving at 26 MS/s: a 5 dB, 3.4 ms gain step every 102.4 ms

## The question

2026-11-18, while testing tone attribution over the air. Receiver: nano-super (Orin Nano
Super, JetPack 6.2) with B200mini serial 322750B, RFObserver 0.15.0b0 plus
feat/tone-attribution, 915 MHz centre, 26 MS/s, gain 35. Transmitter: `ubuntu` (.177)
with B200mini 321D126, 100 ms CW pulses at 915.5 MHz, 1 MS/s, LO offset 1 MHz, gain 40
or 60, over the air in the lab.

Symptoms:

- 3 of 20 strong (-62 dBFS) 100 ms pulses were not labelled Tone. The classifier's
  envelope check measured std/mean 0.107 to 0.114 against its 0.1 limit; a synthetic
  tone measures 0.010.
- Every pulse also produced two 3.5 ms detections at exactly +-3 MHz (912.499 and
  918.504 MHz), 17 dB below the tone. The RFID classifier labelled some of them
  RFID-Reader.
- At gain 40 (near the 30 dB detection threshold) 20 pulses produced 4 detections of
  17 to 63 ms, two of them labelled RFID-Reader.

## The answer

Any B200mini receiving at 26 MS/s (both units tested, two UHD builds) drops the level of
what it receives by 5 dB (to 0.56 in amplitude) for 3.4 ms every 102.4 ms, and during the
drop shows lines 3 MHz either side of the signal. It is in the raw UHD samples, so it is
below RFObserver. The cause inside the radio is NOT known (see "Open").

RFObserver's classifiers were changed to live with it: the tone check judges the level by
its median deviation over 1 ms blocks and needs 15 ms on; the RFID check needs 20 ms and
dips that go nearly to zero.

## The procedure

1. **Archived isolated snippets** (`store/bursts/*.sigmf-data`, the exact bytes the
   classifiers saw) for the three missed pulses and a hit: the misses' line fraction was
   0.997+ and they had no dips; only the envelope check failed. Isolates which check.
2. **Envelope in 1 ms, then 0.1 ms blocks**: each pulse has one step to 0.55 of its
   level, sharp within 0.1 ms, flat for 3.4 ms, carrier frequency unchanged. The step's
   position in the pulse advances about 23 ms per pulse (66, 91, 11, 34, 57, 80 ms...),
   so it is periodic and not tied to the pulses.
3. **Change the transmitter software**: UHD 4.9's `tx_waveforms` instead of the Python
   UHD 3.15 script. Same ghosts, about every 100 ms. Rules out the TX script.
4. **Independent receiver (RTL-SDR on nano-super, 2.4 MS/s)** on the same transmission,
   tuned 200 kHz off so the tone is clear of its DC spike, gain 0 so the 8-bit ADC does
   not clip: envelope std/mean 0.017, no 3.4 ms steps in 1.27 s (12 were due). The
   transmitter is clean.
5. **Raw UHD capture on nano-super, RFObserver stopped** (`rx_samples_to_file
   --args num_recv_frames=1024 --rate 26e6 --freq 915e6 --gain 35`): steps at 100.1,
   202.5, 304.9, 407.8 ... ms, 3.4 to 3.5 ms long, depth 0.55 to 0.57; +-3 MHz lines in
   the same blocks. RFObserver is not involved.
6. **Swap roles** (receive on .177's 321D126 with UHD 4.9, transmit from nano-super):
   same steps, every 102.4 ms, depth 0.57 to 0.58. Not one unit, not one UHD build.
7. **AD936x RX tracking off** (`set_rx_dc_offset(False)`, `set_rx_iq_balance(False)`,
   Python UHD on nano-super), A/B against on: 16 steps off, 19 on, identical shape.
   Not the DC or IQ tracking loops.

## Measured and REJECTED (do not retry)

- **The Python TX script (UHD 3.15 against UHD 4.9 images) causes it**: `tx_waveforms`
  (UHD 4.9) gives the same ghosts every ~100 ms; no underflows in the script's output.
- **The transmitter at all**: the RTL-SDR sees a steady carrier (cv 0.017), and the step
  follows whichever B200mini is receiving (step 6).
- **RFObserver (isolation, channelizer, ring)**: present in `rx_samples_to_file` output
  with RFObserver stopped.
- **One faulty unit or one UHD build**: 322750B (nano-super UHD) and 321D126 (UHD 4.9)
  both show it.
- **AD936x DC offset / IQ balance tracking**: off vs on, identical (step 7).
- **RFObserver polling the radio**: the receiver makes no periodic calls;
  `get_temperature()` is never called.

## Measurement traps hit

- First RTL-SDR capture: the TX was on only in the last 0.4 s of a 4 s window, and at
  20 dB tuner gain the u8 ADC clipped (power +37 dB). Tune off the tone, gain 0, longer
  window.
- First RTL-SDR analysis masked +-2 kHz around DC to skip the RTL's DC spike, which is
  where the tone was, and locked onto the TX LO leakage at +903 kHz instead.
- A tracking A/B that started the TX 5 s after launching the capture saw no tone: UHD
  setup took most of the window. Start the capture, wait for "streaming", then transmit.
- A 30 ms crop of a weak fragment was no longer claimed by RFID while the full snippet
  was; the RFID false positive depends on which part of the fragment is seen. The test
  fixture is the crop that reproduces it (the last 30 ms).
- My `grep -o U` underflow count matched "UHD" in the banner lines.

## Open, not yet answered

- What in the B200mini sets a 102.4 ms period and a 3.4 ms, 5 dB step with +-3 MHz
  lines. Not yet tried: other sample rates (20, 30.72 MS/s) and master clock rates, other
  RX gains, `num_recv_frames` defaults, a B210.
- Whether the HCRO sensors (B200mini/B205mini, 26 MS/s) show it. Expected yes; would show
  as 3.5 ms detections 3 MHz either side of strong carriers (e.g. RFID reader hops).
- Whether the ghosts are worth suppressing in the detector (they are real samples, 17 dB
  down, 3.4 % of the time).
